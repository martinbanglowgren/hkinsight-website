About

Founder & Advisor

My name is Martin Bang-Löwgren and I am the founder of HK Insight, based in Copenhagen. I advise clients on finance, M&A, investment strategy, corporate strategy and sustainability / sustainable finance, drawing on more than 25 years across private equity, investment banking, management consulting and corporate strategy.

Most recently, I spent 16 years as a Director at Polaris, a Copenhagen-based private equity firm, and served as its first Head of Sustainability from 2020 to 2025 — building the firm's approach to ESG integration, climate strategy and EU regulatory readiness across SFDR, CSRD, the EU Taxonomy and CSDDD.

Before my time in private equity, I gained relevant experience from roles in corporates, management consulting and corporate finance.

All my engagements draw on this combination of investment, strategy and corporate experience and sustainability expertise to support clients across a broad range of topics.

Engagement model

How we work together

I have the ability to support on project management, process, decision-making and communication, combined with analysis and research. I am capable of engaging with stakeholders at all levels of a firm, covering the high-level questions but also willing and able to engage in the actual work.
