About

Founder & Advisor

Martin Bang-Löwgren is the founder of HK Insight, based in Copenhagen. He advises clients on corporate finance, M&A, investment strategy, business strategy and sustainable finance, drawing on more than 25 years across private equity, investment banking, management consulting and corporate strategy.

Most recently, he spent 11 years as an investment professional at Polaris, a Copenhagen-based private equity firm, before becoming its first Head of Sustainability from 2020 to 2025 — building the firm's approach to ESG integration, climate strategy and EU regulatory readiness across SFDR, CSRD, the EU Taxonomy and CSDDD.

Engagements draw on this combination of investment experience and sustainability expertise to support clients across the full deal and strategy lifecycle.
